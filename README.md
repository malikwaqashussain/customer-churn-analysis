# 📊 Telco Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI Customer Churn Dashboard** built to analyze telecom customer behavior, identify the key drivers of churn, and uncover high-risk customer segments.

The goal of this project was to transform raw customer data into a clear, business-focused dashboard that helps stakeholders understand **who is leaving, why they are leaving, and what actions could improve retention**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective
<img width="2559" height="1062" alt="Churn Analysis by waqas" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/102bfe6d-dec1-4702-a606-6f374be4bf04" />


Customer churn directly impacts recurring revenue and customer lifetime value.

This project focuses on answering key business questions:

* What is the overall customer churn rate?
* Which customer segments have the highest churn?
* How does churn vary by age and gender?
* Which services are associated with higher churn?
* What are the primary reasons customers leave?
* Which customer groups should be prioritized for retention?
* What actions could help reduce customer churn?

## 🔍 Dashboard Analysis

### 👥 Demographic Analysis

The dashboard analyzes churn across different demographic groups, including:

* Gender
* Age Group
* Marital Status
* Customer characteristics

### Key Finding

**Female customers showed a churn rate approximately 65% higher than male customers.**

Customers aged **50+** also represented an important high-churn segment.

This highlights the need to investigate whether specific customer needs, service expectations, pricing, or product preferences are contributing to higher churn within these groups.

# 🚨 Key Churn Reasons

The analysis identified several important **competitor-driven churn reasons**:

* Competitors made better offers
* Competitors had better devices
* Competitors offered more data
* Competitors offered higher download speeds

These findings suggest that competitive positioning is an important part of the retention problem.

---

# 💡 Business Takeaways

The analysis suggests that the business should move beyond a generic retention strategy.

### 1. 🎯 Build Targeted Offers

Develop specific offers for high-churn demographic and age groups, particularly customers aged **50+**.

### 2. 📦 Improve Service Bundles

Create more competitive packages combining:

* Internet
* Data
* Devices
* Support
* Entertainment services

### 3. 📱 Improve Device Options

Competitors offering better devices was identified as a churn driver, suggesting opportunities around:

* Device upgrades
* New device plans
* Competitive hardware bundles

### 4. 🚀 Improve Internet Value

Review:

* Download speeds
* Data allowances
* Internet packages
* Pricing

against competitor offerings.

### 5. 💰 Strengthen Retention Offers

Identify customers showing signs of churn and provide targeted incentives **before they switch to competitors**.

## 🧠 Analytical Framework

The project demonstrates the following approach:

```text
Customer Data
      ↓
Data Cleaning & Transformation
      ↓
Customer Segmentation
      ↓
Churn Analysis
      ↓
Identify Churn Drivers
      ↓
Find High-Risk Segments
      ↓
Retention Strategy
```

The objective is to move from:

> **"How many customers are leaving?"**

to:

> **"Who is leaving, why are they leaving, and what should we do about it?"**
